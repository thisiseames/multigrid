How MULTIGRID works

You bring the streams. MULTIGRID arranges them. Video goes straight from each provider to you.

Twitch
Channel, video and clip links. Twitch only plays on sites it's told about, so MULTIGRID adds this site's address automatically: .
YouTube
Watch, youtu.be, live, Shorts and embed links. A channel name like @name/live can't be played elsewhere, so copy the link from the stream's Share button. For a channel's newest videos, paste its channel ID: on the channel's page, click …more, then Share channel, then Copy channel ID. YouTube allows one video at a time to start on its own, so other YouTube tiles wait for a click.
Kick
Channel links and player.kick.com links. Muting reloads the tile, because that's the only control Kick's player offers, unless the MULTIGRID Sound extension is installed.
TikTok
Links to one video. A link to an account, like tiktok.com/@name, shows TikTok's card of its recent videos, which play when you point at them. Short links like vm.tiktok.com can't be opened from here, so open them and copy the full address.
X
Links to a post. The tile shows the whole post with its video. Its sound needs the MULTIGRID Sound extension. Short t.co links need the full address too.
Your files
Drop or choose video files in Add stream. They play from this computer and are never uploaded. Chrome can reopen them next visit after a click. Other browsers ask you to choose the file again. MP4 with H.264 is the safest format.
.m3u8
Direct HLS streams. The stream's server has to allow outside players, and the link must be https.
Iframe code
Only the address in src is used. The rest of the code is ignored and never run.
Anything else
Tried as it is. Many sites refuse to play inside other sites, and a browser usually can't tell MULTIGRID when that happens. If a tile stays blank or shows a refusal, the site is blocking it. Its sound needs the MULTIGRID Sound extension.

Sound

The speaker in the top bar opens the mixer, a row with every stream by name. Mute or unmute one, or change its level, without hunting for it in the grid.

Pasted iframe code and links from other sites keep their sound to themselves. In Chrome on a computer, the MULTIGRID Sound extension lets the mixer reach them, and gives Kick and TikTok a level too. iPhones set levels with the phone's own buttons, so the mixer shows mute only there.

Arranging

Drag a tile's top bar onto another tile to swap them. Choose Custom in the layout menu to drag the lines between tiles, or drag a tile's corner to change how much space it takes.

Twitch asks for tiles of at least 400 × 300. Smaller tiles may need a click to start.

Saving

Click a tile's star to name it and keep it in Favorites, at the top of Add stream, with an optional folder. Click a filled star to change it. Your favorites and layouts are kept in this browser on this device, and aren't shared with your other devices.
