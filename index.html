How MULTIGRID works

You bring the streams. MULTIGRID arranges them. Video goes straight from each provider to you.

Twitch
Channel, video and clip links. Twitch only plays on sites it's told about, so MULTIGRID adds this site's address automatically: .
YouTube
Watch, youtu.be, live, Shorts and embed links. A channel name like @name/live can't be played elsewhere, so copy the link from the stream's Share button. For a channel's newest videos, paste its channel ID: on the channel's page, click …more, then Share channel, then Copy channel ID.
Kick
Channel links and player.kick.com links. Muting reloads the tile, because that's the only control Kick's player offers, unless the MULTIGRID Sound extension is installed.
TikTok
Links to one video. A link to an account, like tiktok.com/@name, plays its latest videos one after another, with Back and Next in the tile's top bar. Any public account works: the first time, the tile looks it up (a few seconds) and keeps the list for six hours. Popular accounts are already built in. To change account, click the account's name in the tile's top bar and pick another; the tile keeps its place. Short links like vm.tiktok.com can't be opened from here, so open them and copy the full address.
X
Links to a post. The tile shows the whole post with its video. A link to an account, like x.com/@name, plays its latest videos one after another, with Back and Next in the tile's top bar; X only shows its latest five posts to a logged-out reader, so the list is short. Sound needs the MULTIGRID Sound extension on a post, and works normally for an account feed. Short t.co links need the full address too.
Your files
Drop or choose video files in Add stream. They play from this computer and are never uploaded. Chrome can reopen them next visit after a click. Other browsers ask you to choose the file again. MP4 with H.264 is the safest format.
.m3u8
Direct HLS streams. The stream's server has to allow outside players, and the link must be https.
Iframe code
Only the address in src is used. The rest of the code is ignored and never run.
Anything else
Tried as it is. Many sites refuse to play inside other sites, and a browser usually can't tell MULTIGRID when that happens. If a tile stays blank or shows a refusal, the site is blocking it. Its sound needs the MULTIGRID Sound extension.

Today

Today shows your teams' next games, one-off TV events on your channels, movies opening in cinemas or premiering on streaming this week, new episodes of your series and top headlines. Weather comes from Open-Meteo. Movie releases come from Wikipedia's film lists, ranked by how many people are reading about each film, and trailers from YouTube.

TV guide

Guide in the top bar shows what's on now and next for favorites that are TV channels, matched by their names, like CNN or Sky Sports Premier League. Favorites also show what's on beside their names. Shows, YouTube channels and streaming services have no schedule. Times are in this device's time zone.

Sound

The speaker in the top bar opens the mixer, a row with every stream by name. Mute or unmute one, or change its level, without hunting for it in the grid. One sound, Ticker and Mute all sit in the ⋯ menu at the end of the bar.

Pasted iframe code and links from other sites keep their sound to themselves. In Chrome on a computer, the MULTIGRID Sound extension lets the mixer reach them, and gives Kick and TikTok a level too. iPhones set levels with the phone's own buttons, so the mixer shows mute only there.

Arranging

Drag a tile's top bar onto another tile to swap them. Choose Custom in the layout menu to drag the lines between tiles, or drag a tile's corner to change how much space it takes.

Twitch asks for tiles of at least 400 × 300. Smaller tiles may need a click to start.

Saving

Click a tile's star to name it and keep it in Favorites, at the top of Add stream, with an optional folder. Click a filled star to change it. Your favorites and layouts are kept in this browser on this device. To copy your favorites to another device, click Send to phone beside Favorites and open the link it gives you there. An app added to an iPhone's Home Screen keeps its own favorites, separate from Safari. To fill it, copy the link and paste it into Add stream there.
