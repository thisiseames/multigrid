# MULTIGRID product map

Last audited: Nov 21, 2026, with three full passes (core UI/Add stream, sources/players, Today/Guide/Sports/Reader/jobs/extension). Keep it current when a feature ships.

Read this before proposing anything. It is the inventory of what the site already does. If an idea overlaps something here, improve that thing or explain why the new idea is materially different. Do not pitch a lesser version of a feature that already exists.

Live at https://thisiseames.github.io/multigrid. Plain ES modules, no build step. One owner; laptop Chrome and iPhone Safari/home-screen app. Favorites, layouts and places are per device and can sync.

## Shell and navigation

- **Desktop top bar:** MULTIGRID wordmark (saved layouts), Home/Streams toggle (shows the home screen without clearing the wall), layout picker, channel-surf arrows, Today, Reader, Sports, Search (⌘K), More, Add stream. More holds Saved layouts, Phone remote, Sync favorites, One sound, Ticker, Game mode, Mute/Unmute all, Fullscreen, How it works. The top bar is dark chrome and every control/icon is pure white.
- **Phone:** top bar has the wordmark, Home/Streams, Search, Today, Sound (when streams exist), “N hidden” (when needed) and Add/Stream; Layout, Reader, Sports, Guide, Remote, Saved, Help, Fullscreen and More are hidden there. Bottom tabs are Watch, Reader, Sports, Guide (only when there is guide content) and Remote. Panels become sheets. The always-on phone stream bar sits above the tabs with name, now/next, prev/next channel, play/pause and mute. Tap targets are 44pt. The home-screen app is standalone (manifest), with safe-area handling; it has separate storage from Safari.
- **Home (nothing playing, or the Home icon):** wordmark, billboard and rows Continue watching, Recent grids, Your teams, New today, On now, **The Multigrid Ten**, Top 10 on Netflix · Canada and Trending in Canada. Edit home reorders or hides rows and picks the banner parts. On a phone the same home opens from the top-bar Home/Streams icon, with a shorter billboard and phone-sized cards; an empty phone still starts on the wall until Home is tapped. Desktop behaves as before.
- **Event Room:** from a tile’s ••• menu or Fix stream, **Event Room** finds every feed for the same event and opens a 2–4 feed room. Sports use the same game graph as Fix stream (favourite channels first, then other channels/providers, then feeds already on the wall); TV/news use exact Guide titles with overlapping time; either way, the tile’s own feed is main and has the sound. The room panel takes the Game mode/X-Ray column: event name, score/clock or show time, feed chips, Sound/Main, Re-tune, Add feed and Exit. Clicking a chip makes it main and gives it the sound. The room is saved in Saved layouts with a **ROOM** badge and re-tunes when reopened; Exit/Undo restores the previous wall. A dead event shows **Not live · saved feeds** and keeps the feeds. Rooms are laptop-only; iframe feeds are grouped, never sync-played.
- **X-Ray:** click a tile's hover card (laptop; X-Ray column) or the tile ••• menu → X-Ray (phone; sheet). Shows: picture, IMDb/RT scores, a dedicated **Season recap** from `recaps.json` (when YouTube has one; season-aware, one per Series favourite), this episode, previously on, **Extras** (trailers, promos, cast interviews from extras.json; grouped Trailers/Promos/Interviews, and a Recap only when there is no dedicated card), cast, next episode. Films (Atlantic /movie/ links, or titles IMDb calls films): poster, year · runtime · genres, director, the story (Wikipedia, ~3 sentences + link), scores, Extras (Wikidata's official trailer if the job has none), cast. Games: score, win chance, Replays (ESPN clips), scoring. **Tap any face** in a cast → that person's page inside X-Ray (back arrow): photo, one line, Known for (titles their Wikipedia intro names, one per franchise), More with <name>; every title has a poster, IMDb score and Play (Atlantic, or where you left off from Favorites/Continue watching). Trailers/extras/replays play in a tile with the sound and close themselves at the end (phone: in the sheet). Code: src/xray.js, src/films.js (Wikidata/Wikipedia), app.js xray* functions; plan notes/plans/xray-films-extras.md.
- **Layouts:** Single, Equal, Vertical stack, Horizontal row, Grid and phone, Feature and phone, Big screen and phone, Picture in picture, Picture in picture ×2, 2×2, 3×2, 2×3, 3×3, 4×4, Featured, Focus and row, One and two, One over two, Two and three, Two beside three, Pinwheel, Custom. Saved layouts, current layout, primary tile, add/replace/fill/remove, fullscreen, pop-out, Cinema, Crop.
- **TV mode:** More → TV mode fills the wall with the pictures: each tile's strip, hover card, resize corners and custom dividers are hidden, but the app bar stays. Each player covers its cell, so only a 1 px seam shows. Remembered per device (`multigrid.tv.v1`); phones keep their strips.
- **Desk (laptop):** More → Desk keeps the wall beside one real panel — Today, Reader, Sports (Games, Standings, Transfers, Team), Guide or Search. Drag the divider (300–620 px); the open state, width and last panel are remembered. The panels are the same elements as the popovers and dialogs, not copies; clicking a story, game or channel opens it on the wall. One right column at a time: Game mode, X-Ray and Event Room replace Desk, and Desk replaces them. The phone keeps its sheets.
- **Sound:** desktop mixer row under the top bar with back/play/forward, mute and volume for each controllable tile; One sound (default on); Ticker (default off, bottom only); Mute all/Unmute all; podcast dock. Phone Sound sheet and per-tile sound actions. Tiles start muted so autoplay works; the first click restores saved sound. Sound controls use white/near-white, never periwinkle.
- **Tiles and hidden streams:** title strip, star/favorite, primary crown, Cinema, Crop, mute, fullscreen, reload, pop-out, Cast/AirPlay, Send to phone, replace, open site, remove, more. The **Fix stream** icon in the laptop tile strip, or **Fix stream** in the more sheet, opens source memory: the game the tile is showing, its channels/providers, the same channel's backups, and Games on now. It replaces in place; same-channel repairs update the favourite and keep its star, different-channel repairs leave the old favourite alone. A “N hidden” top-bar button lists streams that don't fit the current layout; choose one to swap it in or remove it. Feed tiles add Previous/Next and a counter. Playlists add Previous/Next episode. PiP tiles use Swap screens; PiP×2 has a Swap in each small mixer row and the phone stream sheet.
- **Search palette (⌘K):** Favorites, saved layouts, PPV events and actions at rest. After two characters it also searches live channels (Catalog + Zlive merged by name, with a source picker), live matches (PPV.st, Daddy Live, Matchora), and your teams' games (Sports). Clicking adds through the same paths as the existing pickers. It does not search Guide shows, Reader stories, podcasts or the local library. Typing paints on a 140 ms debounce; Enter and arrows flush at once. Normal search does not build the merged games list (Fix stream still does); this keeps typing responsive.
- **Help:** in-app How it works dialog.

## Add stream

- **Paste** a URL, iframe code, playlist, local file, note, box score, reader link, or audio link. Add. The Add button disables at 16 streams. The paste Add and X account Add are the normal dark raised buttons; tabs/chips and focus use white/near-white, never periwinkle.
- **X account** field (fixed `x.com/@` prefix, 1–15 characters) with Add; it auto-saves to the Twitter folder.
- **Action row** is Find a live channel, Play a video file from this computer/phone, Browse PPV events, and Import a list — four across on a wide laptop, 2×2 under 980px, one column on phone.
- **Import a list** opens a chooser: Import an X list; Import YouTube subscriptions (Google Takeout `.zip` or `.csv`, 1,500-channel cap, already-saved channels disabled); Import TikTok following (TikTok `Following.txt` or the JSON export). YouTube channels land in the YouTube folder; TikTok follows land in the TikTok folder (an existing folder is reused, any capitals, and a renamed account keeps its name). YouTube/TikTok follows sync unless this device has **Sync YouTube and TikTok follows** switched off. The old stacked links are gone. On phones, Add stream still hides Import a list by the owner’s Nov 23 request; laptop and iPad show it.
- **Files:** one file becomes one tile; several files become one episode playlist in filename order (100-file cap), with auto-advance, Previous/Next, per-episode resume, one Continue watching card. Chrome keeps file handles; Safari asks again after reload.
- **On touch phones** Add a note is hidden in Add stream; Find a live channel stays in the top action row. Channel Finder is also reachable from Search actions.
- **Find a live channel:** the dialog has two sources: **Catalog** (the job-built verified IPTV list; 566 channels in the current file) and **Zlive** (277 live channels from `zlive.st/embed/manifest.json`, CORS-open, no key). Zlive rows show flag, region, category and quality; search and region/category chips; adding uses its clean player with white controls, the app background and no Zlive title/branding. Every Zlive link is normalized on play too, including manually pasted ones and older favorites, so the purple frame becomes the clean white player; Zlive iframes also hide the player page’s purple scrollbars. Links can stop working.
- **Live events:** Browse PPV events opens a three-tab picker: **PPV.st** (live/upcoming events with posters; saves in the PPV folder), **Daddy Live** (live-only, keyless `daddylive.app/api/events`, 60-second cache; one link adds directly, multiple open Choose a link; saves in Daddy Live; its wrapper is cropped to hide the 47-player bar) and **Matchora** (keyless CORS-open `matchora.to/api/v1/schedule?watchable=1`; live/today/tomorrow filters, sport/category chips; one match card adds its clean frameable `embed_url`; saves in Matchora; the player carries Matchora's ads). Links can die or be geo-blocked.
- **Local Library:** one LIBRARY tab inside Favorites with TV / MOVIES switch. TV folder groups shows/seasons/episodes, TVmaze posters, TVmaze episode titles/air dates/synopses, Next up, episode playback, refresh/new downloads, Change/Forget TV. Movie folder has flat movie cards, year, 2:3 portrait cards six across on desktop, local `poster.jpg`/`folder.jpg`/`cover.jpg` → Wikipedia theatrical poster → frame → film icon, single-file playback and resume, a Details pill with Wikipedia/Wikidata plot, cast and runtime, Change/Forget movies. Chrome laptop only; hidden on phone/Safari. Refresh rescans (and it auto-refreshes every 5 minutes while open); old index upgrades automatically; a TV episode playlist is a snapshot, so new downloads appear after Refresh.
- **Podcast directory:** PODCASTS tab → **Find a podcast**. Before typing, it shows Apple's **Top Shows** (25) and **Trending Episodes** (10) for Canada from the job-built `podcast-charts.json`; tap a top show for its latest episodes and Follow, or a trending episode to play it in the dock. Typing searches Apple's public podcast directory by show or topic; clearing returns the charts. Followed shows are hidden favourites (only in PODCASTS, not All or Recent) and sync. Podcast show art is square 1:1 (Apple sends 600×600/3000×3000), not the 16:9 TV/favourite shape. Podcast controls use white/near-white, never periwinkle. A show already in the six Reader feeds appears once and uses its Reader episodes. No new section.
- **Continue watching:** part-watched streams/podcasts/files, newest first; playlist places dedupe to one card. Progress bars are white/near-white, including YouTube, never periwinkle.
- **Favorites:** 342 in the owner's current setup across Sports, Series, Twitter, Entertainment, YouTube, News, TikTok, X, Streaming and unfiled. Folder tabs are All, Recent (last 10), user folders A–Z, Podcasts, Library (Chrome desktop) and Saved layouts on phone. Search covers favorites, podcast shows and the library. Cards have add, ••• Edit/Remove with a second confirm, folder rename with merge; selecting a folder shows its own bar with Rename and Delete, and Delete removes the whole folder and its favorites after a count-bearing confirm, with Undo. Favorites and Library show cards are five across on a wide laptop, three at 1100px, two at 820px and two on the phone; movie cards stay six across. TikTok/X account feeds are kept out of All. Sync/Synced button; no CSV/JSON export button (Sync is the path), though legacy `#fav1=` transfer links are still accepted.
- **Notes:** text tile that saves with the layout.
- **Sync favorites:** pairing code, encrypted favorites/layouts/places via Nostr relays. On a phone, open **Add stream → Favorites** and tap **Sync**; that section is now shown even on a fresh phone with no favorites. **Sync YouTube and TikTok follows** is a per-device checkbox, on by default: checked, the YouTube folder and TikTok profile follows travel and a new device receives them; unchecked (for setting up someone else’s MG), they stay on the device they are on, are never sent or added from another device, and are not deleted by another device’s sync. The **X folder always stays local** either way. Sync setup (while not syncing) also has **Clear Continue watching on this device (N)** for a handoff: it clears this device's places, keeps local-file places, and leaves other devices' history alone. A normal YouTube video saved outside the YouTube folder and a TikTok video link still sync; only profile follows are controlled by the checkbox.

## Sources and players

- **Workspace limit:** 16 streams per workspace; anything beyond the chosen preset is listed under the “N hidden” button, not played.
- **Source memory:** a tile can remember its game (key, label, start) and its channel. Game links live on the current tile, not the favourite, and expire after 8 hours; saved layouts keep the stream and source links but drop the game link. Favourite backups are same-channel only, max 6, newest first, deduped by URL; they sync with favourites and can be removed from Fix stream. The Zlive URL identifies the real channel even when the tile is labelled just “Sky Sports”; a generic family label never guesses between games. Iframe health stays manual.
- **X-Ray:** series, live TV and game detail in the Game mode/X-Ray column (a sheet on the phone). Series get a poster, this episode, previously on, cast, next episode, scores and **More like this** — real JustWatch neighbours under **“Because you’re watching …”**, up to five compact rows with backdrop, year, IMDb/RT and **Open in Atlantic**. JustWatch is fetched by the MULTIGRID Sound extension (the page cannot call it: no CORS), cached per show for seven days; same-kind only, current show excluded, must have a picture and score, and the current show’s genres must overlap enough to drop weak matches. If the extension is missing/old or JustWatch doesn’t answer, the section stays hidden. Games get score, win chance, Replays and scoring plays.
- **Matchday post-match:** the full-time card holds the home banner for 2 hours after full time (hover previews other cards, then returns); the card and the Post-match Game mode tab show the score, goals, stats, table move, win-chance graph, next match and a Reaction section. The Post-match tab replaces the live feed with Reaction → Analysis → Next match. Reaction pulls official/broadcaster YouTube, ESPN and Reader plus the fan channels Stretford Paddock, Stephen Howson, Lyes, Culture Cams, Adikastakes, MinaFootball and No Question About That; `matchday-reactions.yml` refreshes the fan feed every 5 minutes around a game, while the full highlights job stays about 25 minutes. The home screen is laptop-only.
- **YouTube:** watch, Shorts as single videos, live, embeds, playlists; channels saved as uploads feeds (`/channel/UC…/videos`) using the videos-only list so Shorts are excluded, with newest uploads, Back/Next and counter; oEmbed titles; unplayable recovery and list skip. Only a canonical `/channel/UC…` link/ID or a Share link resolves a channel; `@handle`, `/c/` and `/user/` are rejected in-app (the extension resolves them on site). No CC control.
- **Twitch:** channels, VODs, clips; live status and previews from api.ivr.fi; Guide Twitch strip; VOD seek, live cannot seek; clips have no pause/seek and mute reloads them without the extension. No VOD resume, chat, or live-first sorting.
- **TikTok:** 22 built-in feeds plus any public account via r.jina.ai; account switcher; Following import from the export file; vertical 9:16 tile/phone column; feed Back/Next and auto-advance. TikTok live cannot be embedded, so `/@name/live` opens the profile feed. Account favorites are kept out of All.
- **X/Twitter:** single posts and per-profile video feeds with account switcher and HTML fallback; “Import an X list” creates individual Twitter-folder profile favorites, **not** one combined list timeline. No scrollable X browser, X-list feed, or single-post pause/seek.
- **Kick:** live channel embeds only; no live status, preview art, schedule, resume or native VOD/clip support (those links become website tiles).
- **HLS and direct links:** only a `*.m3u8` link gets the native/hls.js player with mute, volume, pause, ±10 s, fullscreen, crop/Cinema and casting. A plain `.mp4`/`.webm` link is a website iframe, not the native player. Live HLS never resumes; recordings over 10 minutes do.
- **Iframe websites:** any other http(s) URL or pasted iframe becomes an unsandboxed website tile. Sound/pause/seek need the extension; a series' current page and in-frame resume also need it. Refusals cannot be detected, so there is no “may be blocked” label.
- **HLS backup feeds:** when a live HLS channel fails, MULTIGRID maps it to other iptv-org feeds for the same channel, tests up to six in the browser, and uses the first that plays. Channel Finder also test-plays a channel before adding it. The backup is not saved into the tile.
- **Local files/playlists:** one file is one tile; multiple files become one playlist tile in filename order, capped at 100, with Previous/Next, `n/N`, auto-advance, per-episode resume and one Continue card. Files never upload; Chrome keeps handles, Safari asks again.
- **Audio/podcasts:** the dock handles art, title/show, back/forward, play, speed, mute, draggable seek, close, resume and lock-screen controls. Sources are the Reader's six feeds and the Apple podcast directory (full `episodeUrl` audio, not 30-second previews), with search and follow. Only a Reader/extension/directory pick creates `audio:`; a pasted `.mp3` is a website tile. One episode at a time, no queue or auto-next.
- **Reader stories:** encrypted full text tile, summary fallback, read state; no resume or sound.
- **Box scores and notes:** box-score tiles from Sports with live refresh; note tiles save with the layout. Neither takes favorites/sound/resume.
- **Series pages:** a website series remembers the episode and position (extension page tracking); reload, pop-out and Continue open that episode; favorite cards show `S2 E8 · 34 min in`; new-episode date/badge from the TVmaze/episode jobs.
- **Resume/Continue:** only content at least 10 minutes long, watched past 1 minute and before 95%; live never. Covers YouTube, HLS recordings, local files/playlists, series pages and podcasts; per-device, places sync.
- **Pop-out:** Chrome Document Picture-in-Picture for any stream except Reader via pop.html; Safari can float only a plain video; hidden from the iPhone home-screen app.
- **Cast/AirPlay:** only HLS tiles; Chrome/Edge Google Cast, Safari AirPlay; appears when a TV is found. No casting for YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, X, Kick or iframe tiles.
- **Remote/phone:** pair once; phone controls the laptop (streams, pause/mute/volume, primary, Cinema, PiP Swap screens, channel surf with Prev/Next channel, reload, add/replace, One sound, Ticker, presets, add stream, copy favorites). A main Play/Pause transport at the top controls the big stream, else the primary, else the first pausable one, and shows its name and Playing/Paused; each stream card keeps its own pause button. The remote's Add stream shows **Continue watching** from the screen, with poster, progress and time left; tapping one opens it at its saved series page on the laptop. Local files are included: the laptop still holds their file handles. If the list is empty, the section says so; if the laptop is on an older build, it asks for one reload. It has no Crop, pop-out, cast, Send to phone, true fullscreen or Custom layout. Send to phone sends one stream with its place; files and playlists are excluded. On a phone, the **Remote** tab opens **Connect remote** with a paste field: copy the link from the laptop’s More → Phone remote, send it to the phone, and paste it once. The QR always opens Safari, so the Home Screen app uses the paste field; after pairing it reconnects by itself. The remote header has **Guide**: a now/next list for the screen’s TV channels, and **Watch** adds that channel on the screen. PiP and PiP×2 tiles get **Swap screens** in their remote card.

## Today / Guide / Sports / Reader

- **Today:** date/weather (Open-Meteo), headlines (Reader), your teams, events today, movies this week, new episodes, premieres, charts, flight deals from Toronto, prediction markets (Polymarket), trending on X, newest YouTube uploads, Continue watching, recent podcasts, markets ticker (CNBC). It opens fresh every time on the Today view and the Hot 100 chart.
- **Today / This week switch:** “This week” means tomorrow through the next seven days, not Monday–Sunday. It swaps today's teams/events for “Your teams this week” and “Events this week”, and hides headlines, trends, Continue watching, podcasts, YouTube uploads, PPV, prediction markets, flight deals and charts. Do not treat those sections as all-week features.
- **Charts:** Billboard charts (Hot 100, Billboard 200, R&B/Hip-Hop, Latin, Afrobeats), movement markers. Row actions already: **Watch here** (YouTube video), **Spotify**, **Amazon Music**, **YouTube Music** when no video. There is no 30-second preview; do not propose one as new.
- **The Multigrid Ten:** a laptop-home row after On now, ten films and shows ranked on the device from favourites, part-watched places and finished titles (`multigrid.watched.v1`), plus IMDb/Rotten Tomatoes scores. The pool is the job-built `catalog.json` from JustWatch (six genres plus the overall list); `src/for-you.js` does the taste, the 50/30/15/5 ranking and the four-per-genre cap. Every card opens Atlantic: exact saved title first, otherwise Search in Atlantic. The row has a banner switch, off by default; a missing or older-than-seven-days catalogue hides it. The old Today **Top 10 in Canada** section is gone; the two home rows **Top 10 on Netflix · Canada** and **Trending in Canada** stay, and `top10.json` still supplies their Rotten Tomatoes scores and trailers.
- **Guide:** TV listings for favorite TV channels with Now and Timeline views, folder pills (Sports then a Man Utd folder of Man Utd shows), search over title/description, show details, Watch here; Twitch live strip; Channel Finder. The Guide button/tab hides when there are no TV listings and no Twitch favorites. The guide job covers roughly now−30 min to now+30 h and refuses to run unless the guide is at least 165 minutes old.
- **Sports:** Games, Standings, Transfers and Team, plus Game mode as a separate 330 px laptop column (never over a stream) with Scores/Odds; on a phone Game mode becomes the bottom ticker. **Transfer desk** (`src/transfers.js`, `transfers.json`): United's done deals from the Premier League's own transfer centre (player, direction, club, date, details link), one latest deal per club, the ESPN squad by position with average ages, and United transfer stories from the Reader with source tiers. It shows the next window countdown and the reported £ fees from the club season page (Baleba £65m, Santos £48m, Tielemans £35m, Højlund £43.2m; £152m spent, £53.2m received). The official PL feed has no fees, so no fee is invented. **Team desk** (`src/team.js`, `team.json`): a crest snapshot and depth bar, three availability cards with photos, position tabs (one table at a time), contract-year pills and a loans strip; the 30 current players by position with age, contract expiry, market value (≈GBP from Transfermarkt), availability and this season’s minutes/goals/assists/points from FPL, team news, contracts by year and loans out; contracts from Transfermarkt, no market values. *Transfer desk layout:* a lead deal card, spent/received bar with net, a six-row Done table, three Reader links and a five-card league strip; “See all 30” opens the full list. **Matchday programme** (`src/programme.js`): opened from the build-up/full-time card and the phone matchday sheet; opposition form, table place, danger man, United’s last XI and out list, last meeting, venue and TV. No referee or stadium weather (no reliable free feed). Leagues in `src/leagues.js` (PL, LaLiga, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, UCL, Nations League, NFL, NBA) feed Sports, Standings, Game mode, odds and the ticker. Highlights for finished games (official channels), lineups board, Polymarket odds, PPV picker. Game rows already have Watch on a favorite, box score as a tile, and highlight buttons. **Matchday** (src/matchday.js, Man United): on the team's match day the laptop home banner leads with the game early in the day, holds it from 2 h before kick-off (or the TV coverage start) through full time (countdown, form, table, win chances, the channel from the guide, Watch, Matchday grid; the XI on a pitch once line-ups are in; live score, last event, stats), then a full-time card in the rotation for the rest of the day; Build-up / Reaction rows (club YouTube from highlights.json `club`, ESPN videos as direct .mp4, Reader Man United stories); other days a slim Next line. Match centre = first Game mode tab on the laptop (score, Watch, win chance graph, timeline, stats, line-ups, commentary), a sheet on the phone (from the Sports panel's Matchday line or the ticker).
- **Reader:** feed tabs (All, News, Sport, Man United, Entertainment, Tech, Travel, Podcasts & Video), unread counts, Mark all as read, read dimming, story view. There is **no Reader search**; the ⌘K palette does not search stories. Full article text is encrypted with a private reader key, with an unlock link; a story opens as a Reader tile, YouTube tile or podcast dock. Podcast/YouTube feeds feed the audio shelf/Today.
- **Movies this week:** Wikipedia posters, trailers via YouTube, cinema/streaming label.
- **Flights:** daily job, Google Flights via `google-flights-ts`, links to booking.
- **Weather, markets, predictions, trends:** free keyless services; each hides quietly when unavailable.

## Data, jobs and the extension
- extras.json (job-owned): guide.yml runs tools/build-extras.mjs (rules in tools/extras-pick.mjs) after the Top 10s: YouTube trailers/recaps/promos/interviews for the Top 10s, Today's premieres and movies, and the series in notes/setup-data.json favorites; ~weekly per title, 25 titles and 8 minutes a run, no key.

- **Jobs:** `.github/workflows/guide.yml` (guide, feeds, movies, charts, shows, season recaps; about every 30 min but skips until the guide is 165 min old), `reader.yml` (Reader self-chaining every 25 min with a 45-min stale backup; also highlights, the transfer desk and the Team desk), `flights.yml` (daily), `channels.yml` (daily), `podcasts.yml` (Apple Top Shows/Trending Episodes, hourly), `catalog.yml` (The Multigrid Ten catalogue, daily), `scores.yml` (IMDb scores, Mondays), `highlights.yml` (manual), `matchday-reactions.yml` (fan reactions every 5 minutes). Job-owned JSON: `guide.json`, `feeds.json`, `charts.json`, `shows.json`, `movies.json`, `flights.json`, `live-channels.json`, `highlights.json`, `transfers.json`, `team.json`, `podcast-charts.json`, `catalog.json`, `recaps.json`, `scores/*`, `reader/index.json`, `reader/a/*`. Never hand-edit or commit these; run the job and discard the output when testing.
- **Outside services:** Open-Meteo, CNBC quotes, TVmaze, Wikipedia/Wikidata, billboard.com, YouTube oEmbed/search, ESPN, Twitch previews/api.ivr.fi, r.jina.ai, Polymarket Gamma, iptv-org, Google Flights, PPV.st, Daddy Live (`daddylive.app/api/events`), Matchora (`matchora.to/api/v1/schedule`), Zlive (`zlive.st/embed/manifest.json`), JustWatch (Top 10, The Multigrid Ten and X-Ray’s similar shows), Apple Podcasts (iTunes Search API) and Apple podcast charts (Marketing Tools RSS, read by the hourly job). All free/keyless. No paid service.
- **Storage/sync:** localStorage for favorites, workspaces, resume/played/places, settings; IndexedDB for local file handles and the local library; sync encrypted on free Nostr relays; favorites transfer links.
- **Extension MULTIGRID Sound (1.16):** background content script in all frames for sound, pause/play, seek, in-frame resume, page tracking, and progress; toolbar popup Send this tab (v1.11) scans the page for YouTube video/channel, direct video/audio/player/page, resolves real UC… channel IDs, and sends to MULTIGRID. The top-frame bridge also asks the background for JustWatch’s similar shows when X-Ray opens (the page cannot call JustWatch directly). Owner reloads the extension after a version bump.

## Platform limits and rules that shape proposals

- The iPhone home-screen web app has separate storage from Safari. A link opened from Mail/Safari does not share the home-screen app's favorites; use Sync or the transfer link. The Chrome extension does not exist in Safari or the home-screen app.
- The X folder is always local to each device. The YouTube folder and TikTok profile follows are local only on a device where **Sync YouTube and TikTok follows** is unchecked; otherwise they sync normally. A paired device not showing the other device’s X folder, or its YouTube/TikTok items when the checkbox is off, is correct, not a sync failure. Folder names are matched exactly (any capitals). Moving a favorite out of X/YouTube makes it sync again when the checkbox is on; a TikTok profile link is controlled by the checkbox, but a TikTok video link is a normal favorite.
- The local TV/movie library and persistent local file handles are Chrome-laptop only. Safari asks for local files again after a reload. iPhones can play direct audio/video links but not a local folder library.
- Twitch stops playback when anything covers its player. No overlay, toast or menu may sit on a Twitch picture; use the bars/sheets. Twitch needs roughly 400×300.
- On iPhone the mixer volume sliders have no effect; the phone's own buttons set volume. Upright phones stack every layout into one column; sideways phones use the normal presets.
- Browsers start tiles muted so autoplay works; sound is restored by a click/One sound. Do not propose autoplay with sound.
- Cross-origin iframes and embeds may refuse to play, need a login, be geo-blocked, or not work on iOS. The app fails quietly or offers a replacement/backup feed; it does not bypass DRM or logins.
- An Event Room is laptop-only because the wall is laptop-only. It groups feeds and keeps one sound; it does not synchronise iframe playback, and a feed without a playable source is left out.
- JustWatch’s API has no CORS header, so X-Ray’s **More like this** only works when MULTIGRID Sound 1.16+ is installed and reloaded. Without it, or if JustWatch doesn’t answer, the section is hidden; there is no genre-only fallback.
- GitHub Pages is HTTPS: HTTP media/embeds are blocked as mixed content. Prefer HTTPS.
- The repo is public. Secrets live in GitHub secrets/local files and are never committed.
- Apple's podcast directory is free/keyless and CORS-open, but Apple limits it to about 20 calls a minute; the app caches searches for 10 minutes, a show's episodes for 30, and calls only on search/open. The Canada store misses some shows; premium/region-locked episodes have no playable `episodeUrl` and are hidden. Apple's chart feed has **no CORS**, so `podcasts.yml` caches it hourly; a chart older than 48 hours is hidden.
- Every outside service is free and keyless; paid services, accounts and keys need the owner's explicit yes.
