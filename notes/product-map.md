# MULTIGRID product map

Last audited: Nov 21, 2026, with three full passes (core UI/Add stream, sources/players, Today/Guide/Sports/Reader/jobs/extension). Keep it current when a feature ships.

Read this before proposing anything. It is the inventory of what the site already does. If an idea overlaps something here, improve that thing or explain why the new idea is materially different. Do not pitch a lesser version of a feature that already exists.

Live at https://thisiseames.github.io/multigrid. Plain ES modules, no build step. One owner; laptop Chrome and iPhone Safari/home-screen app. Favorites, layouts and places are per device and can sync.

## Shell and navigation

- **Desktop top bar:** MULTIGRID wordmark (saved layouts), Home/Streams toggle (shows the home screen without clearing the wall), layout picker, channel-surf arrows, Today, Reader, Sports, Search (⌘K), Reels, Live, TikTok, More, Add stream. More holds Saved layouts, Phone remote, Sync favorites, One sound, Ticker, Game mode, Mute/Unmute all, Fullscreen, How it works. The top bar is dark chrome and every control/icon is pure white.
- **Phone:** top bar has the wordmark, Home/Streams, Search, Today, TikTok and Add/Stream. Reels, Live and Sound moved into the wordmark/MULTIGRID menu (Live opens the phone Live sheet; Sound opens the sound sheet). Layout, Reader, Sports, Guide, Remote, Saved, Help, Fullscreen and More are hidden there. Bottom tabs are Watch, Reader, Sports, Guide (only when there is guide content) and Remote. Panels become sheets. The always-on phone stream bar sits above the tabs with name, now/next, prev/next channel, play/pause and mute. Tap targets are 44pt. The MULTIGRID menu also holds Opening logo and Opening sound switches. A fresh phone launch shows the MULTIGRID wordmark on near-black first, then the plain opening collage, then lands on Home. Streams with no streams shows the logo collage empty wall. Black iPhone launch images stop the white flash before it. The home-screen app is standalone (manifest), fills the whole screen and keeps the bottom stack clear of the home indicator; it has separate storage from Safari.
- **Home (nothing playing, or the Home icon):** wordmark, billboard and rows Continue watching, Recent grids, Your teams, New today, On now, **The Multigrid Ten**, Top 10 on Netflix · Canada, Trending in Canada and Trending trailers. On the Ten, Netflix and Trending billboards the second button is **Add movie / Add show / Add title** (or **Open movie / show / title** when saved), not Search in Atlantic; one click adds the exact TMDB title to Series or Movies, and an ambiguous title opens the Find a title picker. Nine IMDb/RT rows start directly under **Trending in Canada**: IMDb MOVIEmeter, Hidden Gems, Most Popular TV, Popular on Streaming; RT Certified Fresh, Verified Hot, Best Movies 2026, Best TV 2026, Most Popular TV. Each has 20 landscape 16:9 cards using JustWatch backdrops (text card when there is no backdrop); hovering previews the billboard. When JustWatch has an official trailer or teaser, the billboard leads with **Play trailer** and keeps Add/Open as the second button; otherwise Add/Open leads. The nine rows are also options in Edit home → Banner, off by default like Ten/Netflix/Trending. **Trending on YouTube** is the last Home row, after Trending trailers: US `mostPopular` through the official YouTube Data API, tabs All / Music / Sports / Comedy / Entertainment / News & Politics / Science & Technology / Movies / Shows, 12 landscape cards per tab, Play opens a YouTube tile, Shorts under three minutes and live/upcoming items are excluded, and its banner option is off by default. Edit home reorders or hides rows and picks the banner parts. On a phone the same home opens from the top-bar Home/Streams icon, with a shorter billboard and phone-sized cards; an empty phone still starts on the wall until Home is tapped. Desktop behaves as before. **Daypart home:** More → Daypart home (or the phone’s Edition chip) composes the same rows and banner for morning, afternoon, prime or late, with a Morning brief / On now / Tonight / After hours lead; morning carries cached weather, new episodes lead prime, weekend mornings lead with teams, and matchday still leads. It never changes the wall or normal home, costs $0, and is remembered only in the URL.
- **Event Room:** from a tile’s ••• menu or Fix stream, **Event Room** finds every feed for the same event and opens a 2–4 feed room. Sports use the same game graph as Fix stream (favourite channels first, then other channels/providers, then feeds already on the wall); TV/news use exact Guide titles with overlapping time; either way, the tile’s own feed is main and has the sound. The room panel takes the Game mode/X-Ray column: event name, score/clock or show time, feed chips, Sound/Main, Re-tune, Add feed and Exit. Clicking a chip makes it main and gives it the sound. The room is saved in Saved layouts with a **ROOM** badge and re-tunes when reopened; Exit/Undo restores the previous wall. A dead event shows **Not live · saved feeds** and keeps the feeds. Rooms are laptop-only; iframe feeds are grouped, never sync-played.
- **X-Ray:** click a tile's hover card (laptop; X-Ray column) or the tile ••• menu → X-Ray (phone; sheet). Shows: picture, IMDb/RT scores, a dedicated **Season recap** from `recaps.json` (when YouTube has one; season-aware, one per Series favourite), this episode, previously on, **Extras** (trailers, promos, cast interviews from extras.json; grouped Trailers/Promos/Interviews, and a Recap only when there is no dedicated card), cast, next episode. Films (Atlantic /movie/ links, or titles IMDb calls films): poster, year · runtime · genres, director, the story (Wikipedia, ~3 sentences + link), scores, Extras (Wikidata's official trailer if the job has none), cast. Games: score, win chance, Replays (ESPN clips), scoring. **Tap any face** in a cast → that person's page inside X-Ray (back arrow): photo, one line, Known for (titles their Wikipedia intro names, one per franchise), More with <name>; every title has a poster, IMDb score and Play (Atlantic, or where you left off from Favorites/Continue watching). The same sheet opens from Search → **Find a person** without a tile; that search covers directors, writers, producers and creators as well as cast, and matches Wikidata labels stored as `en` or `mul`. Trailers/extras/replays play in a tile with the sound and close themselves at the end (phone: in the sheet). Code: src/xray.js, src/films.js (Wikidata/Wikipedia), app.js xray* functions; plan notes/plans/xray-films-extras.md.
- **Layouts:** Single, Equal, Vertical stack, Horizontal row, Grid and phone, Feature and phone, Big screen and phone, Screen + two lives, Screen + four lives, Picture in picture, Picture in picture ×2, 2×2, 3×2, 2×3, 3×3, Featured, Focus and row, One and two, One over two, Two and three, Custom. 4×4, Two beside three and Pinwheel are retired from the picker, but screens saved with them still open as they were. Saved layouts, current layout, primary tile, add/replace/fill/remove, fullscreen, pop-out, Cinema, Crop.
- **Live:** the top-bar Live button (after Reels, laptop only) has four tabs: **TikTok · Twitch · Kick · YouTube**. TikTok lists saved live accounts from the **Live** folder; Twitch, Kick and YouTube list the channels in the **Twitch**, **Kick** and **YouTube** folders. Each row shows LIVE / Offline / Checking and viewers where available, opens the channel in a tile, has a small + for another tile and a trash action with Undo. The add box accepts a full URL, `@name`, a plain name or a live link; a pasted link switches to its tab. Twitch uses api.ivr.fi, Kick its public channel data and YouTube its `/live` page through r.jina.ai. A main click keeps one live tile per non-TikTok platform; + adds another. TikTok lives added to a wall still use the shared vertical tile; the Live menu uses the rail below. The top-bar badge counts live channels across all four. TikTok lives stay in **Live**, are kept out of All and old live favourites move there; their cards keep the LIVE/Offline badge. YouTube channel favourites are kept as they are and the menu opens their `/channel/UC…/live` link. **TikTok rows open a Live Room rail beside the wall**: the wall keeps its layout, tiles and players and only narrows. The rail is only the lives, with no title, permanent format row or Add live; its width fits the lives at the stage height. The four formats **1 · 1 beside 1 · 1 over 1 · 2 × 2** and **Close Live Room** × are in the Live menu while it is open. A row click switches the rail's main live; + / an empty slot add another; lives hidden by a smaller format return in 2 × 2. Each rail live has mute/fullscreen/close and starts muted. It is not saved and never replaces or changes the wall; closing it returns full width and a reload closes it. Twitch/Kick/YouTube and Add stream still use the wall while the rail stays open. The **Live now** section is no longer on Today. The Live Room rail is laptop-only. On the phone, the wordmark/MULTIGRID menu has a **Live** row that opens the Live sheet with **Twitch, Kick and YouTube only**; TikTok is left out because it needs the laptop extension, and a pasted TikTok live link is refused with that message. The app checks TikTok/Twitch/Kick about once a minute and YouTube about every five minutes while open; no desktop notifications.
- **Reels:** a website saved in a folder called REELS is a vertical feed. The top-bar Reels menu lists those favorites; opening one uses a 9:16 phone column, like a TikTok feed. There is only one Reel tile at a time: opening another Reel, or saving a new site to REELS while one is open, swaps the source in that tile. The menu opens with a built-in **Trending on Bluesky** row, then the saved favorites in their arranged order: use the up/down arrows to move one, or the trash button to remove it (with Undo). Saving a website into REELS from its favorite box reshapes the current tile; the flag is stored with the layout and survives reload.
- **TV mode:** More → TV mode fills the wall with the pictures: each tile's strip, hover card, resize corners and custom dividers are hidden, but the app bar stays. Each player covers its cell, so only a 1 px seam shows. Remembered per device (`multigrid.tv.v1`); phones keep their strips.
- **Desk (laptop):** More → Desk keeps the wall beside one real panel — Today, Reader, Sports (Games, Standings, Transfers, Team), Guide or Search. Drag the divider (300–620 px); the open state, width and last panel are remembered. The panels are the same elements as the popovers and dialogs, not copies; clicking a story opens it inside Reader (with a back button and Open page link), while games and channels open on the wall. One right column at a time: Game mode, X-Ray and Event Room replace Desk, and Desk replaces them. The phone keeps its sheets.
- **Sound:** desktop mixer row under the top bar with back/play/forward, mute and volume for each controllable tile; each row also has an × that removes that tile. One sound (default on); Ticker (default off, bottom only); Mute all/Unmute all; podcast dock. Phone Sound sheet and per-tile sound actions, with the same × on every stream card. Tiles start muted so autoplay works; the first click restores saved sound. Sound controls use white/near-white, never periwinkle.
- **Tiles and hidden streams:** title strip, star/favorite, primary crown, Cinema, Crop, mute, fullscreen, reload, pop-out, Cast/AirPlay, Send to phone, replace, open site, remove, more. The **Fix stream** icon in the laptop tile strip, or **Fix stream** in the more sheet, opens source memory: the game the tile is showing, its channels/providers, the same channel's backups, and Games on now. It replaces in place; same-channel repairs update the favourite and keep its star, different-channel repairs leave the old favourite alone. A Series tile leads with **This series**: Atlantic, 1Flex and Cinejoy (Cinejoy only on laptop Chrome with MULTIGRID Sound 1.17), each opening the same season and episode in place; the star and Series card stay on the original favourite, and Atlantic returns. Live/game rows are hidden for a series. A “N hidden” top-bar button lists streams that don't fit the current layout; choose one to swap it in or remove it. Feed tiles add Previous/Next and a counter. Playlists add Previous/Next episode. PiP tiles use Swap screens; PiP×2 has a Swap in each small mixer row and the phone stream sheet.
- **Search palette (⌘K):** Actions first, Favorites below and PPV events at rest; saved layouts are no longer in Search (the wordmark/menu still has them). The panel is pure black with a near-black input, matching the current design; it is taller (up to 84vh/760px on laptop, `calc(100dvh - 150px)` on phone) and its results scroll fully to the last PPV row. After two characters it also searches live channels (Catalog + Zlive merged by name, with a source picker), live matches (PPV.st, Daddy Live, Matchora), and your teams' games (Sports). Clicking adds through the same paths as the existing pickers. It also has **Find a show / Find a movie / Find a title**: TMDB's public search through r.jina.ai, with source chips Atlantic (default) and 1Flex for shows; picking a result saves it in Series or the existing MOVIES folder with its card-sized poster and an all-caps name, matching the rest of Favorites, and opens it in a tile; an existing title opens instead of duplicating. For a show the sheet adds at S1 E1; films use Atlantic `/watch/<tmdb>`. If the search fails or is empty, Search Atlantic (and Search 1Flex for shows) open the sites' own search pages. Cinejoy is not in search. Actions include **Find a show**, **Find a movie** (same TMDB search, films save to MOVIES), **Find a person** (TMDB people search; opens the existing X-Ray person sheet, actors and directors/creators) and **TV calendar** (Series favourites, next seven days). It does not search Guide shows, Reader stories, podcasts or the local library. Typing paints on a 140 ms debounce; Enter and arrows flush at once. Normal search does not build the merged games list (Fix stream still does); this keeps typing responsive.
- **Menus and popovers:** the layout picker, MULTIGRID/saved layouts drop, TikTok accounts, Live and More use the current black surface with near-black controls and white active states, not the old grey raised panels. Recent grids are no longer in the MULTIGRID drop (they remain a Home row and on the phone's Layouts tab).
- **Help:** in-app How it works dialog.

## Add stream

- **Paste** a URL, iframe code, playlist, local file, note, box score, reader link, or audio link. Add. The Add button disables at 16 streams. The paste Add and X account Add are the normal dark raised buttons; tabs/chips and focus use white/near-white, never periwinkle.
- **X account** field (fixed `x.com/@` prefix, 1–15 characters) with Add; it auto-saves to the Twitter folder.
- **Action row** is Find a live channel, Play a video file from this computer/phone, Browse PPV events, and Import a list — four across on a wide laptop, 2×2 under 980px, one column on phone.
- **Import a list** opens a chooser: Import an X list; Import YouTube subscriptions (Google Takeout `.zip` or `.csv`, 1,500-channel cap, already-saved channels disabled); Import TikTok following (TikTok `Following.txt` or the JSON export). YouTube channels land in the YouTube folder; TikTok follows land in the TikTok folder (an existing folder is reused, any capitals, and a renamed account keeps its name). YouTube/TikTok follows sync unless this device has **Sync YouTube and TikTok follows** switched off. The old stacked links are gone. On phones, Add stream still hides Import a list by the owner’s Nov 23 request; laptop and iPad show it.
- **Files:** one file becomes one tile; several files become one episode playlist in filename order (100-file cap), with auto-advance, Previous/Next, per-episode resume, one Continue watching card. Chrome keeps file handles; Safari asks again after reload.
- **On touch phones** Add a note is hidden in Add stream; Find a live channel stays in the top action row. Channel Finder is also reachable from Search actions.
- **Find a live channel:** the dialog has two sources: **Catalog** (the job-built verified IPTV list; 566 channels in the current file) and **Zlive** (277 live channels from `zlive.st/embed/manifest.json`, CORS-open, no key). Zlive rows show flag, region, category and quality; search and region/category chips; adding uses its clean player with white controls, the app background and no Zlive title/branding. Every Zlive link is normalized on play too, including manually pasted ones and older favorites, so the purple frame becomes the clean white player; Zlive iframes also hide the player page’s purple scrollbars. Links can stop working.
- **Live events:** Browse PPV events opens a three-tab picker: **PPV.st** (live/upcoming events with posters; saves in the PPV folder), **Daddy Live** (live-only, keyless `daddylive.app/api/events`, 60-second cache; one link adds directly, multiple open Choose a link; saves in Daddy Live; its wrapper is cropped to hide the 47-player bar) and **Matchora** (keyless CORS-open `matchora.to/api/v1/schedule?watchable=1`; live/today/tomorrow filters, sport/category chips; one match card adds its clean frameable `embed_url`; saves in Matchora; the player carries Matchora's ads). Links can die or be geo-blocked.
- **Local Library:** one LIBRARY tab inside Favorites with TV / MOVIES switch. TV folder groups shows/seasons/episodes, TVmaze posters, TVmaze episode titles/air dates/synopses, Next up, episode playback, refresh/new downloads, Change/Forget TV. Movie folder has flat movie cards, year, 2:3 portrait cards six across on desktop, local `poster.jpg`/`folder.jpg`/`cover.jpg` → Wikipedia theatrical poster → frame → film icon, single-file playback and resume, a Details pill with Wikipedia/Wikidata plot, cast and runtime, Change/Forget movies. Chrome laptop only; hidden on phone/Safari. Refresh rescans (and it auto-refreshes every 5 minutes while open); old index upgrades automatically; a TV episode playlist is a snapshot, so new downloads appear after Refresh.
- **Podcast directory:** PODCASTS tab → **Find a podcast**. Before typing, it shows Apple's **Top Shows** (25) and **Trending Episodes** (10) for Canada from the job-built `podcast-charts.json`; tap a top show for its latest episodes and Follow, or a trending episode to play it in the dock. Typing searches Apple's public podcast directory by show or topic; clearing returns the charts. Followed shows are hidden favourites (only in PODCASTS, not All or Recent) and sync. Podcast show art is square 1:1 (Apple sends 600×600/3000×3000), not the 16:9 TV/favourite shape. Podcast controls use white/near-white, never periwinkle. A show already in the six Reader feeds appears once and uses its Reader episodes. No new section.
- **Continue watching:** part-watched streams/podcasts/files, newest first; playlist places dedupe to one card. Progress bars are white/near-white, including YouTube, never periwinkle.
- **MUSIC folder:** a YouTube channel favourite plus its `Artist · Album` playlist favourites show as one artist card. Opening it shows Channel and Albums; each plays in a tile. It is a display grouping inside MUSIC, not a nested folder tree; search and other folders stay flat. The `/#music` pack adds 14 artists and 113 albums with covers in one step; open it on the laptop, then let Sync carry it to the phone.
- **Favorites:** 342 in the owner's current setup across Sports, Series, Twitter, Entertainment, YouTube, News, TikTok, X, Streaming and unfiled. Folder tabs are All, Recent (last 10), user folders A–Z, Podcasts, Library (Chrome desktop) and Saved layouts on phone. Search covers favorites, podcast shows and the library. Cards have add, ••• Edit/Remove with a second confirm, folder rename with merge; selecting a folder shows its own bar with Rename and Delete, and Delete removes the whole folder and its favorites after a count-bearing confirm, with Undo. Favorites and Library show cards are five across on a wide laptop, three at 1100px, two at 820px and two on the phone; movie cards stay six across. TikTok/X account feeds are kept out of All. Sync/Synced button; no CSV/JSON export button (Sync is the path), though legacy `#fav1=` transfer links are still accepted.
- **Notes:** text tile that saves with the layout.
- **Sync favorites:** pairing code, encrypted favorites/layouts/places via Nostr relays. On a phone, open **Add stream → Favorites** and tap **Sync**; that section is now shown even on a fresh phone with no favorites. **Sync YouTube and TikTok follows** is a per-device checkbox, on by default: checked, the YouTube folder and TikTok profile follows travel and a new device receives them; unchecked (for setting up someone else’s MG), they stay on the device they are on, are never sent or added from another device, and are not deleted by another device’s sync. The **X folder always stays local** either way. Sync setup (while not syncing) also has **Clear Continue watching on this device (N)** for a handoff: it clears this device's places, keeps local-file places, and leaves other devices' history alone. A normal YouTube video saved outside the YouTube folder and a TikTok video link still sync; only profile follows are controlled by the checkbox.

## Sources and players

- **Workspace limit:** 16 streams per workspace; anything beyond the chosen preset is listed under the “N hidden” button, not played.
- **Source memory:** a tile can remember its game (key, label, start) and its channel. Game links live on the current tile, not the favourite, and expire after 8 hours; saved layouts keep the stream and source links but drop the game link. Favourite backups are same-channel only, max 6, newest first, deduped by URL; they sync with favourites and can be removed from Fix stream. The Zlive URL identifies the real channel even when the tile is labelled just “Sky Sports”; a generic family label never guesses between games. Iframe health stays manual.
- **X-Ray:** series, live TV and game detail in the Game mode/X-Ray column (a sheet on the phone). Series get a poster, this episode, previously on, cast, next episode, scores and **More like this** — real JustWatch neighbours under **“Because you’re watching …”**, up to five compact rows with backdrop, year, IMDb/RT and **Open in Atlantic**. `similar.json` is job-built weekly for every Series favourite (JustWatch, free; same filter: same-kind only, current show excluded, picture and score required, enough genre overlap). X-Ray uses that file first, so the rows work on the laptop and the phone without the extension; for any other show it falls back to the MULTIGRID Sound extension (the page cannot call JustWatch: no CORS). If neither answers, the section stays hidden. It sits at the end of series X-Ray, after Next episode, so it does not split the show's recap and episode details. Games get score, win chance, Replays and scoring plays.
- **Matchday post-match:** the full-time card holds the home banner for 2 hours after full time (hover previews other cards, then returns), and its Reaction row leads the home rows during that hold. After the hold the Reaction row drops directly below Trending in Canada (or to the bottom if that row is hidden/empty), then leaves at midnight. The card and the Post-match Game mode tab show the score, goals, stats, table move, win-chance graph, next match and a Reaction section. The Post-match tab replaces the live feed with Reaction → Analysis → Next match. Reaction pulls official/broadcaster YouTube, ESPN and Reader plus the fan channels Stretford Paddock, Stephen Howson, Lyes, Culture Cams, Adikastakes, MinaFootball and No Question About That; `matchday-reactions.yml` refreshes the fan feed every 5 minutes around a game, while the full highlights job stays about 25 minutes. The home screen is laptop-only.
- **YouTube:** watch, Shorts as single videos, live, embeds, playlists; channels saved as uploads feeds (`/channel/UC…/videos`) using the videos-only list so Shorts are excluded, with newest uploads, Back/Next and counter; oEmbed titles; unplayable recovery and list skip. Only a canonical `/channel/UC…` link/ID or a Share link resolves a channel; `@handle`, `/c/` and `/user/` are rejected in-app (the extension resolves them on site). A channel feed’s name in its tile bar opens a native channel room: banner, avatar, subscribers, description, latest videos, Shorts and playlists, read through r.jina.ai with no YouTube page in a frame. A video, Short or playlist plays over the same room tile with Back to channel, so browsing never fills the grid; Back to feed returns. The **Live** menu’s YouTube tab reads a channel favourite’s channel ID and opens `/channel/UC…/live`; it also accepts a channel link, `@handle` or live video link when adding. No CC control.
- **Twitch:** channels, VODs, clips; live status and previews from api.ivr.fi; Guide Twitch strip; VOD seek, live cannot seek; clips have no pause/seek and mute reloads them without the extension. No VOD resume, chat, or live-first sorting.
- **Bluesky:** **Reels → Trending on Bluesky**, or paste `bluesky:trending`, for a native 9:16 feed of **English** video posts from Bluesky’s public What’s Hot feed (posts are filtered by their language metadata). It shows the author, avatar, post text, likes and reposts, plays the HLS video directly, and uses Back/Next. Pet/cute posts are filtered out by default; the **Not interested** menu can hide the current account or a word/topic locally (with Undo), and **Clear hidden filters** brings them back. Public AppView, no key/account, cached for five minutes. X-Ray is off for this feed so its card cannot cover the video's own controls and hide menu. A normal bsky.app link still opens the site itself.
- **TikTok:** 22 built-in feeds plus any public account via r.jina.ai; account switcher; Following import from the export file; vertical 9:16 tile/phone column; feed Back/Next and auto-advance. The top-bar TikTok menu lists saved and imported accounts; typing in its username field filters that list live by any part of the handle or name (`60`, `sky`), with Add/Profile still available for a new handle. It has a **Profile** action on every account and beside the username field: it opens the public avatar/name/verified mark, followers, following, likes, video count and all ten videos TikTok's embed lists (cover, views, likes, comments, date) in the tall TikTok viewer, replacing the feed already there. It reads the public profile page plus TikTok's account embed through r.jina.ai, caches for 10 minutes, and never loads TikTok's page or TikViewer banner. Clicking a card plays that video over the profile in the same viewer, using TikTok's own player; **Back to profile** returns without adding a tile. If TikTok can't play a card, the overlay keeps Back and Try again. X-Ray is disabled for TikTok feeds, videos, profiles and lives, because it has no TikTok data and the hover card would cover Back. Works on laptop and phone. A live link (`tiktok.com/@name/live` or `tiktoklive:@name`) plays the broadcast natively on laptop Chrome when MULTIGRID Sound 1.17+ is installed; it shows the avatar, nickname, title and viewer count, with Try again and a clear ended/offline state. The top-bar Live menu and Today use the same live lookup; saved lives are checked while the app is open. A wall made only of TikTok lives fills the stage height in normal mode: each live is a 9:16 column with its strip, the top bar/music/sound stay, and no TV mode is needed. The existing + action adds more live tiles. A live PiP or PiP×2 inset is a 9:16 card that fills; a regular video inset keeps its 16:9 shape. A creator broadcasting a landscape/group scene inside a vertical canvas keeps that canvas's own bars. Reels, Live, TikTok feeds and profiles share one 9:16 vertical tile: picking any of them swaps the source in place. The top-bar TikTok menu lists saved and built-in accounts and accepts a bare username; it opens in that same tile. Phone/Safari has no extension, so live shows the extension message instead; use a live-viewer site there. Account favorites are kept out of All.
- **Music player:** the top-bar **Music** button (after TikTok; the MULTIGRID drop on a phone) opens Artists → Albums → Songs from the MUSIC folder. The artist is its YouTube channel; albums are the `Artist · Album` playlist favourites; the song list reads the album's public YouTube Music page through r.jina.ai (cached ten minutes), falling back to the playlist's small RSS feed if that read fails; Play album is the last resort and fills the list from the player. Volume is a small popover on the speaker icon in the menu and the dock. A thin music dock under the top bar plays the official video/audio with play/pause, previous/next, seek, mute, volume, open and close; the top-bar icon shows playing. Clicking a song starts that song, even when the album is a YouTube playlist: the player address carries the album playlist and the song's playlist position. Starting music pauses the podcast and starting a podcast pauses music. The last track is restored paused; nothing autoplays. The phone uses the MULTIGRID drop and its normal sheet; the Sound sheet also gets a Music card. A one-tap **Music pack** at `https://multigrid.club/#music` adds 14 artist channels and 113 of their albums, with real covers, to MUSIC after a confirmation; existing favorites stay put. The Add stream artist dialog, charts/Apple Music links and YouTube channel room are unchanged.
- **X/Twitter:** single posts and per-profile video feeds with account switcher and HTML fallback; “Import an X list” creates individual Twitter-folder profile favorites, **not** one combined list timeline. No scrollable X browser, X-list feed, or single-post pause/seek.
- **Kick:** live channel embeds with live/offline status, avatar and viewers from Kick’s public channel data through r.jina.ai; no preview art, schedule, resume or native VOD/clip support (those links become website tiles).
- **HLS and direct links:** only a `*.m3u8` link gets the native/hls.js player with mute, volume, pause, ±10 s, fullscreen, crop/Cinema and casting. A plain `.mp4`/`.webm` link is a website iframe, not the native player. Live HLS never resumes; recordings over 10 minutes do.
- **Iframe websites:** any other http(s) URL or pasted iframe becomes an unsandboxed website tile. Sound/pause/seek need the extension; a series' current page and in-frame resume also need it. Refusals cannot be detected, so there is no “may be blocked” label.
- **HLS backup feeds:** when a live HLS channel fails, MULTIGRID maps it to other iptv-org feeds for the same channel, tests up to six in the browser, and uses the first that plays. Channel Finder also test-plays a channel before adding it. The backup is not saved into the tile.
- **Local files/playlists:** one file is one tile; multiple files become one playlist tile in filename order, capped at 100, with Previous/Next, `n/N`, auto-advance, per-episode resume and one Continue card. Files never upload; Chrome keeps handles, Safari asks again.
- **Audio/podcasts:** the dock handles art, title/show, back/forward, play, speed, mute, draggable seek, close, resume and lock-screen controls. Sources are the Reader's six feeds and the Apple podcast directory (full `episodeUrl` audio, not 30-second previews), with search and follow. Only a Reader/extension/directory pick creates `audio:`; a pasted `.mp3` is a website tile. One episode at a time, no queue or auto-next.
- **Reader stories:** encrypted full text tile, summary fallback, read state; no resume or sound.
- **Box scores and notes:** box-score tiles from Sports with live refresh; note tiles save with the layout. Neither takes favorites/sound/resume.
- **Series pages:** a website series remembers the episode and position (extension page tracking); reload, pop-out and Continue open that episode; favorite cards show `S2 E8 · 34 min in`; new-episode date/badge from the TVmaze/episode jobs.
- **Series sources:** a Series favourite can come from Atlantic (`/watch/<tmdb>/<s>/<e>`), 1Flex (`/play?id=<tmdb>&type=tv&season=<s>&episode=<e>&token=…`, the full site page in the tile, no extension) or Cinejoy (`/watch/tv/<tmdb>/<s>/<e>`). All three use the same TMDB number. Cinejoy refuses framing unless MULTIGRID Sound 1.17's tab lift strips the headers; it is laptop Chrome only and has no phone route. Atlantic and 1Flex work on laptop and phone. Fix stream switches the tile between them without adding a tile; the original favourite stays.
- **Movie titles:** a film added from a home billboard or Find a movie is saved in the existing MOVIES folder as Atlantic `/watch/<tmdb>`; movies are Atlantic-only, and 1Flex/Cinejoy movie playback is not built.
- **Resume/Continue:** only content at least 10 minutes long, watched past 1 minute and before 95%; live never. Covers YouTube, HLS recordings, local files/playlists, series pages and podcasts; per-device, places sync.
- **Pop-out:** Chrome Document Picture-in-Picture for any stream except Reader via pop.html; Safari can float only a plain video; hidden from the iPhone home-screen app.
- **Cast/AirPlay:** only HLS tiles; Chrome/Edge Google Cast, Safari AirPlay; appears when a TV is found. No casting for YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, X, Kick or iframe tiles.
- **Remote/phone:** pair once; phone controls the laptop (streams, pause/mute/volume, primary, Cinema, PiP Swap screens, channel surf with Prev/Next channel, reload, add/replace, One sound, Ticker, presets, add stream, copy favorites). A main Play/Pause transport at the top controls the big stream, else the primary, else the first pausable one, and shows its name and Playing/Paused; each stream card keeps its own pause button. The remote's Add stream shows **Continue watching** from the screen, with poster, progress and time left; tapping one opens it at its saved series page on the laptop. Local files are included: the laptop still holds their file handles. If the list is empty, the section says so; if the laptop is on an older build, it asks for one reload. It has no Crop, pop-out, cast, Send to phone, true fullscreen or Custom layout. Send to phone sends one stream with its place; files and playlists are excluded. On a phone, the **Remote** tab opens **Connect remote** with a paste field: copy the link from the laptop’s More → Phone remote, send it to the phone, and paste it once. The QR always opens Safari, so the Home Screen app uses the paste field; after pairing it reconnects by itself. The remote header has **Guide**: a now/next list for the screen’s TV channels, and **Watch** adds that channel on the screen. PiP and PiP×2 tiles get **Swap screens** in their remote card.

## Platform limits and rules that shape proposals

- The iPhone home-screen web app has separate storage from Safari. A link opened from Mail/Safari does not share the home-screen app's favorites; use Sync or the transfer link. The Chrome extension does not exist in Safari or the home-screen app.
- The X folder is always local to each device. The YouTube folder and TikTok profile follows are local only on a device where **Sync YouTube and TikTok follows** is unchecked; otherwise they sync normally. A paired device not showing the other device’s X folder, or its YouTube/TikTok items when the checkbox is off, is correct, not a sync failure. Folder names are matched exactly (any capitals). Moving a favorite out of X/YouTube makes it sync again when the checkbox is on; a TikTok profile link is controlled by the checkbox, but a TikTok video link is a normal favorite.
- The local TV/movie library and persistent local file handles are Chrome-laptop only. Safari asks for local files again after a reload. iPhones can play direct audio/video links but not a local folder library.
- Twitch stops playback when anything covers its player. No overlay, toast or menu may sit on a Twitch picture; use the bars/sheets. Twitch needs roughly 400×300.
- On iPhone the mixer volume sliders have no effect; the phone's own buttons set volume. Upright phones stack every layout into one column; sideways phones use the normal presets.
- Browsers start tiles muted so autoplay works; sound is restored by a click/One sound. Do not propose autoplay with sound.
- Cross-origin iframes and embeds may refuse to play, need a login, be geo-blocked, or not work on iOS. The app fails quietly or offers a replacement/backup feed; it does not bypass DRM or logins.
- An Event Room is laptop-only because the wall is laptop-only. It groups feeds and keeps one sound; it does not synchronise iframe playback, and a feed without a playable source is left out.
- JustWatch’s API has no CORS header, so X-Ray’s **More like this** only works when MULTIGRID Sound 1.16+ is installed and reloaded. Without it, or if JustWatch doesn’t answer, the section is hidden; there is no genre-only fallback.
- GitHub Pages is HTTPS: HTTP media/embeds are blocked as mixed content. Prefer HTTPS.
- The repo is public. Secrets live in GitHub secrets/local files and are never committed.
- Apple's podcast directory is free/keyless and CORS-open, but Apple limits it to about 20 calls a minute; the app caches searches for 10 minutes, a show's episodes for 30, and calls only on search/open. The Canada store misses some shows; premium/region-locked episodes have no playable `episodeUrl` and are hidden. Apple's chart feed has **no CORS**, so `podcasts.yml` caches it hourly; a chart older than 48 hours is hidden.
- Every outside service is free and keyless; paid services, accounts and keys need the owner's explicit yes.
