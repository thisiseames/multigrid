# Man Utd Matchday: plan (Nov 22; owner said go; built Nov 22)

## 1. Running cost
$0 a month. ESPN match data, Polymarket odds, Man Utd's YouTube feed and the TV guide are free, keyless, fetched by the browser. Only meter: internet data. While the match centre is open it re-checks ESPN every 30 s (live file ~400 KB uncompressed, ~40–60 KB as sent) → roughly 5–10 MB per match, plus ~1 MB Polymarket. Phone checks only while the match centre is open.

## 2. What he sees
**Timing (owner's change):**
- Earlier on match day: the game is the first item in the banner's normal rotation, alongside everything else.
- From 2 hours before kick-off (or when the TV coverage starts, if earlier): the build-up takes over the banner (11:30 am game → from 9:30 am; 3:00 pm game → from 1:00 pm).
- After full time: the full-time card stays in the rotation for the rest of the day (not holding the banner).
- Other days: a slim countdown to the next game ("Next: Man Utd v Brentford · Sat 10:00 am · in 5 days").

**Build-up (laptop banner):** both crests on a dark backdrop, venue · kick-off time with countdown, form (last 5 each side), league positions, win chances, "Live on Sky Sports Premier League from 11:00 am" (from his guide). Buttons: Watch the build-up (opens the favorite carrying it), Matchday grid. A Build-up row under the banner: ESPN match videos (play in a tile), Man Utd YouTube uploads, Reader Man United stories. Line-ups in (~1 h before): pitch graphic with formations.

**During (laptop):** a Man Utd tab first in the Game mode column: score and clock, timeline (goals, cards, subs with minutes), stats as white bars (possession, shots, on target, corners, fouls, saves), win-chance graph (Polymarket, every minute), line-ups with subs, ESPN minute-by-minute commentary newest first.
**During (phone):** tapping the Man Utd score in the ticker opens the same match centre as a sheet.

**After:** full-time card: result, scorers with minutes, 4 key stats, win-chance story, table movement. Buttons: Highlights (existing), Reaction (club post-match videos, ESPN reaction, Reader match reports).

## 3. Defaults (each "I decided X, change it?")
1. Team: Man Utd, stored as a "Matchday team" setting (any soccer team in MY_LEAGUES later).
2. Timing: as above (rotation earlier in the day; takes over 2 h before kick-off or at TV coverage start; full-time card rotates the rest of the day).
3. Banner holds only during the build-up window through full time; hovering other cards still previews them.
4. Matchday grid: opens the Sky channel carrying the game (from the guide; PL before Main Event) as one large tile and turns on Game mode with the Man Utd tab; replaces the screen, with Undo.
5. Match centre: a tab in the Game mode column (laptop), a sheet from the ticker (phone).
6. Refresh: ESPN 30 s, Polymarket 60 s, only while live and the page is visible.
7. Look: white controls on black; red only inside crests/photos.
8. No goal flashes, sounds or pop-ups.
9. No phone home screen (he passed on it); phone Matchday lives in the ticker sheet and Sports.
